About Our Platform

Empowering Neighborhoods With Transparency

FixIt Local is a civic complaint marketplace created by Team Binary Brains. We bridge the gap between frustrated residents and local service providers across municipal and domestic utility sectors.

Instead of unanswered helpline phone calls, every issue filed on FixIt Local is logged into an open public dashboard. Providers receive structured notices and are motivated to resolve complaints promptly to safeguard their public performance score.

  • Verified Ticket Logging: Photo evidence upload and timestamped tracking for every complaint.
  • Independent Provider Ratings: Fair scores based on verified resolution time and customer feedback.
  • Public Community Board: Full transparency so neighbors can confirm if local outages are widespread.

Simple 3-Step Process

How FixIt Local Works

Resolving neighbourhood utility problems is straightforward with our three-step accountability framework.

  1. 1

    Report

    Fill out our simple form with category, location, and photo evidence. Your ticket enters the verified moderation queue.

  2. 2

    Track

    Watch the service provider investigate the issue, post official progress notes, and confirm when service is restored.

  3. 3

    Compare

    Review response times and resolution percentages across all utility providers to make informed decisions for your home.
